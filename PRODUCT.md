# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three kinds of visitor, in rough order of volume.

1. Engineers who land on a blog post from search, a newsletter link, or social. They want to read the post without friction, then maybe look around.
2. People checking BK out: hiring managers, collaborators, people who met him at a meetup. They want to know what he's built and what he thinks, fast.
3. Indie-hacker and AI-tooling people who come for a project (mxr, FaithBench, blah.chat) and stay for the writing.

Most traffic is on phones and laptops, often at night. Long-form reading is the primary job on the site.

## Product Purpose

bhekani.com is BK's home on the web. It holds his writing (posts, micro posts), his projects, and his reading log. It exists so that the first impression of BK is something he built himself, in his own voice, rather than a LinkedIn profile. Success means people read to the end of a post, click through to a project, or subscribe to the newsletter.

## Brand Personality

Three words. Built, direct, curious.

BK is a civil engineer turned software engineer who likes reducing complexity. The voice is one engineer talking to another. Opinions stated plainly, numbers over adjectives. The site should feel handmade and considered, not templated. It can be playful in the details (sound, haptics, motion) while the reading surface stays calm.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev-portfolio templates. Hero with a gradient blob, identical project cards, tag pills, "Hi, I'm X 👋".
- Cream or beige "literary" body backgrounds with a display serif. That is the current site and the current AI default.
- Terminal cosplay. Green-on-black, monospace body text, fake prompts.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, bento grids, tracked uppercase eyebrows above every heading.
- Anything that slows down reading a post.

## Design Principles

1. Reading comes first. Every choice on a post page is judged by whether it helps someone finish the post.
2. Handmade over templated. Asymmetry, a real colour commitment, details you notice on the second visit.
3. Feedback is physical. Clicks make a small sound and a small buzz. Quiet, optional, never in the way.
4. One strong voice, not many treatments. The same palette and type system carry the whole site.
5. Fast and still. Static HTML, small scripts, motion that ends quickly and respects reduced-motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at 4.5:1 or better in both themes. Large text at 3:1 or better.
- Full reduced-motion alternative for all animation.
- Sound and haptics only ever fire in response to a tap or click, never on load. A visible toggle mutes them and the choice persists.
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus on every interactive element.
- Both light and dark themes are first-class. The visitor's OS preference is respected by default.
